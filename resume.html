Deep Learning · Course Project

Driver Drowsiness Detection Using Multi-Feature Facial Landmarks

Hybrid multi-feature fusion framework combining geometric facial features (EAR, MAR, head pose) from dlib's 68-point landmark predictor with deep CNN representations (MobileNetV2 + EfficientNetB0), fused via AUC-weighted probability ensembling.

  • Multi-strategy face detection pipeline (HOG → upsampled HOG → Haar cascade fallback)
  • Two-phase transfer learning on 2,226 real driver-facing images
  • Outperforms state-of-the-art CDLM Hybrid (99.6%) and DrowsyDetectNet (99.2%)
  • Comprehensive ablation, calibration & robustness analysis; cross-dataset on NTHU-DDD

Deep Learning · Reproduction Study

Deep Learning for Text Summarization: Transformer Reproduction & Cross-Domain Analysis

Four-phase reproduction and extension of a transformer-based news summarization study (Krishna et al., 2025). Identified a critical hardware reproducibility gap, implemented a TF-IDF baseline (suggested as future work), and conducted zero-shot cross-domain transfer to XSum.

  • Reproduced T5-large, BART-large-CNN & PEGASUS-large on 4× NVIDIA V100 GPUs — all scores exceeded original
  • Documented T5-base training collapse (FP16 instability at lr=3e-4) not reported in the original paper
  • Zero-shot cross-domain: all models lost >49% ROUGE-1 on XSum; T5-large generalised best (−49%)
  • METEOR ranking reversal: BART ranked last on ROUGE yet first on METEOR — confirms semantic vs. lexical distinction
  • Fine-tuned BART on XSum recovered 69.3% of ROUGE-1 drop

Natural Language Processing · Reproduction Study

Multimodal Emotion Recognition & Sentiment Analysis in Multi-Party Conversations

Full three-assignment reproduction and extension of Farhadipour et al. (2025) on the MELD dataset (Friends TV show). Implemented all four modality pipelines (text, audio, video, face), proposed gated attention fusion, and conducted cross-domain transfer from IEMOCAP.

  • Reproduced four unimodal pipelines: RoBERTa (text), Wav2Vec2 (audio), MobileNetV2+Transformer (video), InceptionResNetV1+BiLSTM (face)
  • Full multimodal fusion: 64.01% emotion, 87.21% sentiment accuracy on 15% MELD subset
  • Gated attention fusion experiment revealed text modality collapse (99.98% gate weight) — quantified modality dominance
  • Cross-domain IEMOCAP → MELD transfer: fine-tuning improved emotion by +12.34pp and sentiment by +15.64pp over zero-shot
